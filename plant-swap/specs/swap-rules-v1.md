# Swap rules — v1

The rules for swap day. Published on the site and read aloud at the
start of each swap. Changes need the organisers' agreement and a
DECISIONS entry; bump to v2 rather than editing this file.

## What you can bring
- Cuttings, rooted or not, labelled if you know the plant.
- Seedlings and small potted plants.
- Seeds in labelled envelopes.
- Clean spare pots and saucers.

## What you can't bring
- Anything with visible pests or disease.
- Plants on the local weed or invasive species list.
- Anything for sale. No money changes hands.

## How the day runs
1. 10:00 — set out your plants on the tables, sorted by type.
2. 10:15 — rules read aloud, then browsing starts.
3. Take roughly what you brought. Newcomers with nothing to swap may
   take one plant.
4. 11:30 — leftovers go to the library's community garden.

## Labels
Masking tape and markers are on the welcome table. Write the plant
name and any care tips.
