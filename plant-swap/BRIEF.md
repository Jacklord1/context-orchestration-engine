# Plant Swap — BRIEF

## What this is
A free monthly plant swap at the local library. People bring
cuttings, seedlings and spare pots and leave with something new. A
small website gives the date, the rules and an RSVP form.

## Who it serves
Local home growers, mostly renters with balconies and small
gardens. Secondary: the library, which wants community events with
predictable numbers.

## What done means
This is ongoing, so "running well" means:
- a swap every month, booked at least four weeks ahead
- 15+ RSVPs, with the headcount sent to the library three days
  before
- under two hours of organiser time per swap, excluding the day

## Why this matters
Plants are expensive and cuttings are free. It's also the easiest
way for neighbours to meet who otherwise wouldn't.

## What this is NOT
- Not a marketplace. No money changes hands, ever.
- Not a social network. No accounts, no profiles, no feed.
- Not a nursery. Nothing pest-ridden; see the swap rules.

## Constraints
- $0 budget. Free tiers only.
- Two volunteers with day jobs.
- The library room holds 40 people.

## Ask before
- Sending anything to the newsletter list.
- Contacting the library.
- Changing the swap rules (specs/swap-rules-v1.md).
